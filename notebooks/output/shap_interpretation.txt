SHAP Interpretation for Fraud Detection Models

Fraud_Data.csv:
- Key drivers of fraud (based on SHAP summary plot):
  - signup_to_purchase_sec: Short time between signup and purchase is a strong indicator of fraud, suggesting rushed or automated behavior.
  - purchase_value: Unusually high or low purchase values contribute significantly to fraud predictions.
  - user_txn_count: High transaction frequency may indicate suspicious activity.
  - is_country_top10: Transactions from high-risk countries may have higher fraud associations.

creditcard.csv (processed as fraud_features.csv):
- Key drivers of fraud (based on SHAP summary plot):
  - signup_to_purchase_sec: Similar to Fraud_Data, short signup-to-purchase times are critical.
  - purchase_value: Extreme values indicate potential fraud.
  - purchase_hour/purchase_dayofweek: Unusual transaction timing may signal fraud.
  - user_txn_count: Frequent transactions are a key indicator.

Business Recommendations:
1. Implement real-time monitoring for transactions with short signup_to_purchase_sec.
2. Flag transactions with extreme purchase_value or high user_txn_count.
3. Incorporate geolocation-based risk assessment using is_country_top10.
4. Monitor transactions for unusual purchase_hour or purchase_dayofweek patterns.
